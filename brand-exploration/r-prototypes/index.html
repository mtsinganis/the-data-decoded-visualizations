One font. A flexible chart frame.

Work Sans and #2455FF are confirmed identity choices. The lightly simplified pterosaur is the preferred trial mark. This page compares the retained categorical palette A with one cleaner proposal B. These are direct R exports, separate from the live website and historical charts.

Palette A / B

A retains the previous eight colors with confirmed blue and preferred crimson. B keeps those anchors and gives amber, sky, sage, and slate lighter or quieter roles. Both use the same data, category order, wording, and chart frame below. B is recommended for a real-project trial, subject to review; neither is color-blind safe by declaration. Twelve and sixteen categories remain optional stress tests.

Colors are frozen in palette.json. Simulated colors and Oklab nearest-pair distances are diagnostics, not accessibility scores. C2 and C3 use outside labels in both compositions because A cannot give either ink or white text 4.5:1 contrast on those fills. All other inside labels use whichever of ink or white has higher measured contrast.

Measured frame on direct R exports

Work Sans title, optional subtitle, sources and notes, and a left-aligned pterosaur/channel signature share one adaptive frame. Long titles can step down from 35 to 31 pt in portrait (30 to 27 pt in landscape) before adding lines; short titles remain at the maximum. Plot labels never shrink to make room. Non-highlighted filled bars remain provisional #A6B0BD. Open the SVG for editable chart geometry and embedded Work Sans fonts.

The selector changes the preview of selected exported PNGs. SVG links always open the original chart. Simulations are approximate and do not certify accessibility.
