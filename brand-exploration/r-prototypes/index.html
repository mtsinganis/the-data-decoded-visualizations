Palette in context

Recommended eight-color core for further testing: use crimson #C83242 as the fourth color. It kept more separation than vermilion in the earlier protanopia and deuteranopia stress checks and has stronger text contrast on paper. This is a proposal, not a color-blind-safe claim. The two accents are shown in identical charts below; 12 and 16 colors remain optional stress tests.
