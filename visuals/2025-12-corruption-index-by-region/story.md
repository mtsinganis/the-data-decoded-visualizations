This chart compares regional Corruption Perceptions Index scores from 2012 to 2024. The index runs from 0, perceived as highly corrupt, to 100, perceived as very clean.

## Sources and methodology

The source is Transparency International's Corruption Perceptions Index (2024). The original analysis starts in 2012 because the CPI changed from a 0–10 to a 0–100 scale that year. Region assignments follow the groupings shown in the chart's inset map. The processing steps are in the [original Quarto analysis](https://github.com/mtsinganis/the-data-decoded-visualizations/blob/main/visuals/2025-12-corruption-index-by-region/index.qmd).
