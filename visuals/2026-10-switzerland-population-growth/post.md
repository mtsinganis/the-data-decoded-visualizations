Switzerland’s natural increase fell from 38,405 in 1971 to 6,249 in 2025. Net migration reached 70,432 in 2025—91.9% of the two components combined, before statistical adjustments.

Explore the 55-year picture: https://mtsinganis.github.io/the-data-decoded-visualizations/projects/switzerland-population-growth/

## Image and ALT text

### plots/switzerland-population-growth.png

Diverging stacked columns show Switzerland's annual natural increase and net migration from 1971 to 2025. Natural increase, in warm grey, remains positive but declines from 38,405 in 1971 to 6,249 in 2025. Positive net migration is blue; negative balances are red and extend below zero in 1975–1978 and 1996–1997. Net migration reached −57,847 in 1975, peaked at 139,118 in 2023, and was 70,432 in 2025. Annotations refer to the oil-crisis recession and note that some Ukrainian arrivals in 2022 were counted as permanent residents in 2023. The chart excludes statistical adjustments; ESPOP and STATPOP use different definitions.
