# X thread preparation

The supplied X thread is the editorial reference. The three charts selected for the thread are the national historical series, the 2026 canton ranking, and the 2020–2026 change chart, in that order. The cantonal small multiples are included in the website article as a fourth chart. Posting to X is manual; no post has been published.

## Images and ALT text

### plots/switzerland-housing-vacancy.png

A red line shows Switzerland's dwelling vacancy rate from 1984 to 2026. It drops to 0.43% in 1989, rises to 1.85% in 1998, reaches 1.72% in 2020, and falls to 0.93% in 2026. Changes in census definitions and housing-stock methodology affect historical comparisons.

### plots/switzerland-housing-vacancy-cantons-2026.png

Horizontal bars rank Swiss cantons by their 2026 dwelling vacancy rates. Zug is lowest at 0.20%, followed by Geneva at 0.31% and Obwalden at 0.38%. Jura is highest at 3.35%. A red reference line marks the Swiss national rate of 0.93%.

### plots/switzerland-housing-vacancy-change-2020-2026.png

Bars rank changes in vacancy rates from 2020 to 2026. Rates fell in 25 cantons; Aargau fell the most, by 1.60 percentage points, followed by Thurgau at 1.38 and Valais at 1.36. Jura alone rose, by 0.83 points. The Swiss rate fell 0.79 points.
