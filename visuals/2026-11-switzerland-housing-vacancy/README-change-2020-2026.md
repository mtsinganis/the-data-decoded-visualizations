# Canton vacancy-rate changes, 2020–2026

This chart is generated by `change-2020-2026.R` from the existing cleaned canton and national series. Run it from the repository root with the R and Python SVG-optimization dependencies listed in the project README. It produces `data/canton-vacancy-change-2020-2026.csv`, a 6480 × 4320 PNG at 300 dpi, and an optimized SVG. The two pre-existing charts are not regenerated.

## Data and calculations

The canton inputs are the published FSO rates from the archived canton tables described in [README.md](README.md); the national reference comes from the archived national table. For each series the percentage-point change is `rate_2026 - rate_2020`, and relative change is `(rate_2026 / rate_2020 - 1) * 100`. Both input rates are expressed in percent. All 26 cantons and Switzerland have valid 2020 and 2026 observations, and all 2020 denominators are positive. The chart orders the changes from lowest to highest, breaking displayed two-decimal ties alphabetically by abbreviation. The national rate was 1.72% in 2020 and 0.93% in 2026, a change of −0.79 percentage points (−45.9% relative).

The FSO rates are rounded in the relevant source cells, so small differences and ties should not be overinterpreted. Rates are annual snapshots on June 1 of dwellings offered for long-term rent or sale, not all unoccupied homes. National and canton rates are ratios of vacant dwellings to their respective housing stocks; the national rate is not an unweighted average of canton rates. Both endpoints are after the transition to register-based housing stock, though the underlying census and registers can be revised. The wider historical caveats are documented in the main project README.

## Flags

Twenty-six canton flags and the Swiss flag are cached locally as `data/canton-flags/<lowercase abbreviation>.svg`; chart rendering requires no network access. The square SVGs come from [SVG Flags by Francisco Riordan](https://github.com/ciscoriordan/svg-flags), using the `square/states/ch-<abbreviation>.svg` and `square/countries/ch.svg` assets. The source license and NOTICE are retained in that folder. The R script renders them into small square image grobs at fixed equal physical dimensions; it does not distort their source aspect ratios. Labels and flags are small when the complete 27-column image is reduced to typical social-feed width; open the high-resolution image to inspect details.
