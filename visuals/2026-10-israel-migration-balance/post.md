## Proposed thread

1/ International migration became a drag on Israel’s population growth. Annual net migration was +27,600 in 2023, −18,600 in 2024 and −20,900 in 2025. These calendar-year figures may be revised.

2/ The latest provisional period, late September 2025–early September 2026, covers about 11.6 months: 49,000 arrivals versus 57,000 departures left net migration of approximately −8,000. It overlaps 2025 and is not a directly comparable annual improvement.

3/ Arrivals included about 24,000 new immigrants, 4,000 family reunification arrivals and 21,000 returning Israelis. Total international net migration includes these arrivals as well as Israeli emigrants; it is broader than returning Israelis minus emigrants.

4/ About 182,000 births − 50,000 deaths added 132,000 people, offsetting the migration deficit and leaving growth of 124,000, excluding the foreign population. Negative net migration did not mean population decline. These data do not establish migration motives.

Figures are provisional and rounded; the separately reported foreign population is excluded.
