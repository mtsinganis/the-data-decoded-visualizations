Key Homeland Security Metrics - CBP Encounters

This data dictionary defines and describes the variables included in the CBP Encounters Key Homeland Security Metric (KHSM) Report, which provides data on encounters of removable aliens by U.S. Customs and Border Protection, including unlawful border crossings, inadmissible entries at ports of entry, and expulsions under Title 42.

The OHSS Key Homeland Security Metrics - CBP Encounters Data Dictionary fields include:

  • Fiscal Year
  • Quantity
  • Month
  • Citizenship
  • Region
  • Family Status
  • Enforcement/Administrative
  • Field Office
  • Sector
