The first chart compares where people identifying as Jewish live across England and Wales with each region's share of the overall population. The maps provide a closer view by London borough and ward.

## Sources and methodology

The source is the 2021 Census from the Office for National Statistics. As noted in the original chart, a person could identify as Jewish through religion, ethnic group, or both. The [original Quarto analysis](https://github.com/mtsinganis/the-data-decoded-visualizations/blob/main/visuals/2026-05-03-england-wales-jews-geo/index.qmd) processes regional, borough, and ward data for the three figures.
