Switzerland's net migration peaked at 139,118 in 2023, then fell to 70,432 in 2025. The 55-year series also shows six years of net emigration, including −57,847 in 1975.

FSO figures include changes to permanent-resident status; the statistical method changed in 2011.

## Image and ALT text

### plots/switzerland-net-migration.png

Column chart of Switzerland's annual net migration from 1971 to 2025. The balance was negative in 1975–1978 and 1996–1997, reaching a low of −57,847 in 1975. It rose to 98,167 in 2008 and peaked at 139,118 in 2023, highlighted in blue. It then fell to 82,792 in 2024 and 70,432 in 2025. The 2008 and 2025 bars are dark, and negative bars are muted red. Annotations note the oil-crisis recession, EU-17/EFTA quotas lifted in 2007 and strong labour demand in 2008, and 2022 Ukrainian arrivals counted in 2023. The balance includes changes into permanent-resident status. The 2023 peak partly reflects Ukrainian refugees who arrived in 2022. ESPOP and STATPOP use different population definitions.
