Since 1995, several Asian economies have overtaken resource-rich Australia in economic complexity.

China: #42 to #10. Philippines: #69 to #38. Vietnam: #119 to #44. Australia: #33 to #73.

These rankings describe goods exports, not wealth.

Source: Harvard Growth Lab, HS92.

Bump chart of global Economic Complexity Index ranks, 1995–2024, with rank 1 at the top. All 145 economies appear where observations exist. China, green, rises from 42 to 10 (up 32); Philippines, orange, from 69 to 38 (up 31); Vietnam, purple, from 119 to 44 (up 75). Australia, blue, falls from 33 to 73 (down 40). The other 141 economies are grey. Endpoint labels show ranks and changes. Vertical guides mark years. Four contextual annotations describe China's sophisticated manufacturing, Philippine electronics and semiconductor specialization, Vietnam's diversification into electronics and machinery, and Australia's concentration in minerals and energy. These are broad context, not event dates or causal claims. The index measures the mix of exported goods, not wealth or overall economic performance. Coverage rises from 140 to 145 economies.
