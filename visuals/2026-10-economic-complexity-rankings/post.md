Global Economic Complexity Index rankings, 1995–2024. All 145 economies appear where observations exist. China, green, rises from rank 42 to 10, up 32 places. Australia, blue, falls from 33 to 73, down 40. The other 143 economies are grey. Only the two highlighted 2024 endpoints have points; labels show final ranks and changes. Matching annotations describe China's expansion into sophisticated manufacturing and Australia's exports concentrated in minerals and energy. Rank 1 represents the most complex mix of goods exports. The index does not measure wealth or overall economic performance. Coverage varies from 140 to 145 economies. Source: Harvard Growth Lab, version 12.0, HS92.
