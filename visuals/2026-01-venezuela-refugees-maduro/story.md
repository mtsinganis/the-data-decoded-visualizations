The charts show where Venezuelans were hosted from 2013 to 2025, first as counts by destination and then as shares of the total shown. They describe a point-in-time stock rather than annual departures.

## Sources and methodology

The source is the UNHCR Refugee Data Finder, accessed in January 2026. As the original chart notes, the figures include refugees, asylum-seekers, and other people in need of international protection, but exclude internally displaced people. The [original Quarto analysis](https://github.com/mtsinganis/the-data-decoded-visualizations/blob/main/visuals/2026-01-venezuela-refugees-maduro/index.qmd) prepares the destination-country series and charts.
