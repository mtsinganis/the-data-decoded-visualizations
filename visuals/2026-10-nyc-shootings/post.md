Reported NYC shooting incidents rose to their highest annual totals in 2020–2021, then fell. In the NYPD dataset, 2025 had 874 fewer incidents than 2021 (56% lower). The monthly view shows the timing; neither chart explains causes.

These are incident counts, not people shot. 2026 is shown only through June 30.
