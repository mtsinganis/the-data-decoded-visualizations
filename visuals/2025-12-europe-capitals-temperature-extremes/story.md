The chart places each capital's lowest and highest recorded daily temperature in 2025 side by side, from sub-zero lows to summer highs.

## Sources and methodology

The source is Meteostat daily weather data for 44 European capitals through December 29, 2025. The [original Quarto analysis](https://github.com/mtsinganis/the-data-decoded-visualizations/blob/main/visuals/2025-12-europe-capitals-temperature-extremes/index.qmd) selects capital cities, retrieves daily station records, and plots the minimum and maximum recorded temperatures.
