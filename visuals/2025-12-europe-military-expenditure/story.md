The first chart tracks changes in European military-spending ranks from 2000 to 2024. The second compares military expenditure as a share of GDP in 2024.

## Sources and methodology

Both charts use the SIPRI Military Expenditure Database 2025. The spending-rank chart uses current U.S. dollars. Rankings are relative: a country's position can change when another country's spending changes. The [original Quarto analysis](https://github.com/mtsinganis/the-data-decoded-visualizations/blob/main/visuals/2025-12-europe-military-expenditure/index.qmd) contains the country selection, calculations, and chart construction.
