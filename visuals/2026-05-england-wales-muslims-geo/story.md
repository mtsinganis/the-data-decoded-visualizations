The first chart compares where people identifying as Muslim live across England and Wales with each region's share of the overall population. The maps provide a closer view by London borough and ward.

## Sources and methodology

The source is the 2021 Census from the Office for National Statistics. As the original chart explains, Muslim identity here comes from the Census religion question, including written responses classified by ONS as Muslim. The [original Quarto analysis](https://github.com/mtsinganis/the-data-decoded-visualizations/blob/main/visuals/2026-05-england-wales-muslims-geo/index.qmd) processes regional, borough, and ward data for the three figures.
